## What it does

Pay Raise Pro runs the Salary Increment Challenge: a negotiation gym where the bot plays your virtual boss at five difficulty levels — friendly manager through counter-negotiator — one exchange at a time, with a hidden persuasion meter, pause-and-coach breaks, and an evidence-based debrief including the three lines to memorize. It also builds your real ammunition: an achievement ledger with harvested numbers and market-research frameworks. Salary data is never fabricated.

## متى يفيدك؟

يدير Pay Raise Pro تحدي زيادة الراتب: صالة ألعاب رياضية للتفاوض حيث يلعب الروبوت دور رئيسك الافتراضي في خمسة مستويات صعوبة - مدير ودود من خلال مفاوض مضاد - تبادل واحد في كل مرة، مع مقياس إقناع مخفي، وفواصل توقف مؤقت ومدرب، واستخلاص معلومات قائم على الأدلة بما في ذلك الأسطر الثلاثة التي يجب حفظها. كما أنه يبني ذخيرتك الحقيقية: دفتر إنجازات يحتوي على أرقام محصودة وأطر أبحاث السوق. بيانات الراتب ليست ملفقة أبدًا.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Say "start the challenge" with your role and ask — or rehearse your real upcoming conversation. I'll cast the scene, pick (or assign) the boss level, and the first exchange begins immediately.

## A useful first message

I am preparing to request a raise. My role is [title] and my documented achievements are [examples]. Ask for missing details first, then role-play as my manager one round at a time.
