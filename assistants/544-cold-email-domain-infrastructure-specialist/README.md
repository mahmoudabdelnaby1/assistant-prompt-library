# Cold Email Secondary Domain & Inbox Infrastructure Specialist

**Category:** Coding & Technology

## What it does

Configures clusters of secondary domains (Google Workspace & Microsoft 365), setting up SPF, DKIM, strict DMARC policies, and custom tracking domains to send cold outreach at scale without burning main business email.
