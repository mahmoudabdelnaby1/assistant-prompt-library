# Ad Genius

**Category:** Marketing & SEO  
**Assistant page:** [English](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/assistants/014-ad-genius/) · [العربية](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/ar/assistants/014-ad-genius/)

## What it does

Ad Genius architects Google Ads campaigns from a product brief: goal detection, intent-clustered ad groups with match and negative keywords, full responsive ad copy sets within long-stable field ranges, targeting signals, assets, bidding logic, and a measurement plan. Field limits and policy details are flagged for checking in your account; claims are never fabricated and regulated verticals get compliance cautions.

## متى يفيدك؟

يصمم Ad Genius مخططًا متكاملًا لحملات Google Ads انطلاقًا من وصف المنتج أو الخدمة والهدف والسوق. ينظّم الكلمات المفتاحية في مجموعات حسب نية البحث، ويقترح نصوص الإعلانات والأصول والاستهداف ومنطق عروض الأسعار وخطة القياس. يناسب المسوقين وأصحاب الأعمال الذين يريدون تحويل موجز الحملة إلى خطة قابلة للمراجعة والتنفيذ، مع التنبيه إلى التحقق من حدود الحقول والسياسات الحالية داخل الحساب، وعدم افتراض أرقام أو معلومات لم تُقدَّم.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Describe your product or service, market language, and goal (leads, sales, traffic) — budget if you know it. You'll get a launch-ready campaign blueprint: ad groups, keywords, ad copy sets, targeting, and measurement.

## A useful first message

I run a fictional specialty coffee store in Riyadh and want a Google Search campaign to increase orders. The monthly budget is SAR 5,000 and the audience is people searching for coffee gifts. Propose campaign structure, keywords, ads, and measurement metrics, and flag what to verify before launch.
