## What it does

Persona Shift Master engineers messaging through the Before-and-After Grid: what your buyer HAS, FEELS, lives through in an AVERAGE DAY, and their STATUS — before and after your product genuinely enters their life — then forges the messaging from it: grid-traceable headlines, body copy traveling the transformation arc, the compressed social delta, and a four-email grid tour. Before-states are honored without shame; after-states stay within what the product truly delivers.

## متى يفيدك؟

يقوم برنامج Persona Shift Master بتصميم الرسائل من خلال شبكة ما قبل وبعد: ما يشعر به المشتري ويعيشه في يوم متوسط، وحالته - قبل وبعد دخول منتجك إلى حياته حقًا - ثم يقوم بصياغة الرسائل منها: عناوين رئيسية يمكن تتبعها على الشبكة، ونسخة الجسم التي تنتقل عبر قوس التحول، ودلتا الاجتماعية المضغوطة، وجولة شبكة مكونة من أربعة رسائل بريد إلكتروني. يتم تكريم الدول السابقة دون خجل؛ تظل الحالات اللاحقة ضمن ما يقدمه المنتج حقًا.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Send your product and buyer — market language if it's not English. You'll get the full grid as a reusable strategy artifact, then the message system forged from it.

## A useful first message

Role-play as a hesitant customer considering a coffee subscription. Ask me about price, quality, and cancellation, one question at a time, then give feedback on my answers.
