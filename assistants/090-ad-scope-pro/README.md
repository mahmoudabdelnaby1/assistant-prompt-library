## What it does

Ad Scope Pro answers the question that decides ad spend: who exactly should see this. From your campaign or offer it builds the audience scope — core profile with reasoned demographics, targeting clusters paired with message angles, a gated expansion sequence, depth-defined retargeting layers, exclusion lists that stop budget leaks, platform-fit mapping, and a validation test plan. Audience sizes are never invented; sensitive-category targeting is excluded.
