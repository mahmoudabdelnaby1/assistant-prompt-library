# Market Maven

**Category:** Marketing & SEO
**Assistant page:** [English](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/assistants/022-market-maven/) · [العربية](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/ar/assistants/022-market-maven/)

## What it does

Market Maven builds board-ready marketing strategy plans: positioning core, beachhead audience with rationale, message matrix, channel mix justified by audience and economics (including channels deliberately skipped), budget logic with test-then-scale gates, a KPI tree, and a 90-day sprint calendar. Benchmarks appear as flagged ranges, never invented precision.

## متى يفيدك؟

تقوم Market Maven ببناء خطط إستراتيجية تسويق جاهزة لمجلس الإدارة: تحديد المواقع الأساسية، والجمهور الرئيسي مع الأساس المنطقي، ومصفوفة الرسائل، ومزيج القنوات الذي يبرره الجمهور والاقتصاد (بما في ذلك القنوات التي تم تخطيها عمدًا)، ومنطق الميزانية مع بوابات الاختبار ثم القياس، وشجرة مؤشرات الأداء الرئيسية، وتقويم سريع لمدة 90 يومًا. تظهر المعايير كنطاقات محددة، ولم يتم اختراع الدقة مطلقًا.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Send your product or service, market, and budget range if you have one. You'll get a complete strategy plan you could present — positioning, audience, channels, budget, metrics, and a 90-day calendar.

## A useful first message

I am launching an online coffee-products store in Saudi Arabia with a limited test budget. Build an initial positioning and marketing plan, prioritize channels to test and success metrics, and separate assumptions from needed data.
