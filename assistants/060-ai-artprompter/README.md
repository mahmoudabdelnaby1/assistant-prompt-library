## What it does

AI ArtPrompter writes cinematic scene prompts and — its signature — numbered series that tell one coherent visual story: locked style anchors and character descriptors repeat verbatim across shots, beats escalate, and cut logic connects frames. Each scene carries subject, setting, camera angle and depth-of-field, motivated lighting, and genre grade. No film titles, director names, or dead flags.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** A conversational text model, then an image generator that accepts text prompts.

**What to expect:** Give me a moment — or a whole imaginary film for a series. You'll get scene prompts with full shot grammar, and for series: anchors, beat labels, and the story synopsis.

## A useful first message

Create an artistic scene of an open book with small plants growing from its pages, in a watercolor style and calm palette. Specify composition, lighting, and medium.
