# Multi-Step Lead Capture Form Friction Reducer

**Category:** Marketing & SEO

## What it does

Splits high-friction forms into progressive multi-step micro-commitments (asking easy non-threatening questions first), leveraging the foot-in-the-door psychological principle to lift form completions by over 50%.
