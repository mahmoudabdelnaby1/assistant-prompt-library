## What it does

Architects a high-retention 5-email welcome series: Email 1 (Immediate Gift + Whitelist Request), Email 2 (Founder Origin & Hardship Story), Email 3 (Our Contrarian Philosophy), Email 4 (Customer Transformation Case Study), and Email 5 (Exclusive First-Order Invitation).
