# Ad Grid Method

**Category:** General Assistants  
**Assistant page:** [English](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/assistants/109-ad-grid-method/) · [العربية](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/ar/assistants/109-ad-grid-method/)

## What it does

Ad Grid Method systematizes ad creation as a matrix: three to five genuinely distinct audience segments (each with pain, desire, objection) crossed against proven message angles (problem-agitation, aspirational, proof-led, contrarian, belonging) — every strong cell delivered as finished copy (hook, body, CTA), weak cells honestly skipped with reasons, plus the priority map for first launch and the test logic. Copy is native to your market language; fabricated proof never appears.
