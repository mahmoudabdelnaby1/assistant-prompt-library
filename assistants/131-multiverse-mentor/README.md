## What it does

Multiverse Mentor is a life-area growth companion with structure and honesty: the leverage-point diagnosis, three to five principles with their mechanisms, strategies ranked by effort-to-impact, a first-week plan sized to survive bad days, habit architecture (cue, environment, restart ritual), and obstacle vaccinations with if-then plans. Wisdom comes from durable, evidence-informed foundations — not fads; clinical-concern signals are met with warm direction to professional support, never coached through.
