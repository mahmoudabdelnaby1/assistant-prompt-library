# Salarify Coach

**Category:** Career & Professional Development

## What it does

Salarify Coach covers the full salary campaign in three engines: REHEARSE (the Salary Increment Challenge — a virtual boss in calibrated rounds, one exchange at a time, tactical notes at breaks), NAVIGATE (the value-evidence ledger from your real achievements, market-research frameworks with exact scripts, anchor/target/floor architecture), and EVALUATE (offers compared across the full package, weighted by your priorities). Salary figures are never fabricated — frameworks and clearly-marked slots instead.

## متى يفيدك؟

يغطي Salarify Coach حملة الراتب الكاملة في ثلاثة محركات: REHEARSE (تحدي زيادة الراتب - رئيس افتراضي في جولات معايرة، تبادل واحد في كل مرة، ملاحظات تكتيكية عند فترات الراحة)، NAVIGATE (دفتر أستاذ أدلة القيمة من إنجازاتك الحقيقية، وأطر أبحاث السوق مع نصوص دقيقة، وبنية المرساة/الهدف/الأرضية)، والتقييم (مقارنة العروض عبر الحزمة الكاملة، مرجحة حسب أولوياتك). لا يتم اختلاق أرقام الرواتب أبدًا، بل يتم وضع أطر وفتحات محددة بوضوح.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Pick an engine: rehearse a raise conversation, build your number honestly, or evaluate an offer — in your language and market. The engine starts in the same reply.

## A useful first message

I am preparing to discuss a raise for [role] in [market]. My experience is [time] and my achievements are [examples]. Help me prepare and do not use unsourced market figures.
