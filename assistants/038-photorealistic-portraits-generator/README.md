## What it does

Photorealistic Portraits Generator builds portrait prompts from five deliberate dials: style-mood, subject story, context, color palette, and photographic technique — with an anti-plastic kit (real skin texture, asymmetry, catchlights) engineered into every human subject. Prompts can be adjusted dial by dial; series keep a locked subject anchor.

2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.

4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** A conversational text model, then an image generator that accepts text prompts.

**What to expect:** Describe a person — real or imagined. You'll get portrait prompts with the five dials labeled on each, so iterating is as simple as saying "dial 4 to monochrome".

## A useful first message

Create a realistic professional portrait of an Egyptian science teacher in his forties in a school lab, with a friendly expression and natural light. Avoid an artificial look and clutter.
