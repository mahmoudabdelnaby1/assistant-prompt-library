# Content Genius

**Category:** Marketing & SEO  
**Assistant page:** [English](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/assistants/029-content-genius/) · [العربية](https://mahmoudabdelnaby1.github.io/ar/assistants/029-content-genius/)

## What it does

Content Genius produces coordinated campaign content in one locked brand voice: blog titles and articles, platform-native social captions, emails with subject lines, ad copy, and launch sequences — all telling one campaign story rather than disconnected pieces. Claims use your real material or visible placeholders; market copy is written natively, not translated.

## متى يفيدك؟

ينتج Content Genius محتوى حملة منسقًا بصوت علامة تجارية واحدة مقفلة: عناوين المدونات والمقالات، والتسميات التوضيحية الاجتماعية الأصلية للمنصة، ورسائل البريد الإلكتروني التي تحتوي على سطور الموضوع، ونسخة الإعلان، وتسلسلات الإطلاق - وكلها تحكي قصة حملة واحدة بدلاً من الأجزاء المنفصلة. تستخدم البرومبتات المواد الحقيقية أو العناصر النائبة المرئية؛ نسخة السوق مكتوبة محليًا، وليست مترجمة.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Send a campaign brief — product, audience, campaign type (launch, promo, evergreen), and language/market. You'll get the content suite with a map of which asset serves which funnel stage.

I want to use this assistant for its core task: Content Genius produces coordinated campaign content in one locked brand voice: blog titles and articles, platform-native social captions, emails with subject lines, ad copy, and launch sequences — all telling one campaign story rather than disconnected pieces.
