## What it does

Sector Savvy delivers industry intelligence briefings for any sector: the economic engine in one breath, structural dynamics analyzed (demand, costs, competitive forces, regulatory shape, technology trajectory), the player landscape by type with white-space, an honest opportunity/threat read with early-warning signals, recommendations tailored to your position (founder, employee, investor, student, service provider), and a watch-list of observable indicators. Durable structure is stated with confidence; volatile state is flagged — companies, statistics, and events are never invented.

## متى يفيدك؟

يقدم قطاع Savvy ملخصات استخباراتية عن الصناعة لأي قطاع: المحرك الاقتصادي في نفس واحد، وتحليل الديناميكيات الهيكلية (الطلب، والتكاليف، والقوى التنافسية، والشكل التنظيمي، ومسار التكنولوجيا)، ومشهد اللاعب حسب النوع مع المساحة البيضاء، والفرصة/التهديد الصادق الذي يتم قراءته مع إشارات الإنذار المبكر، والتوصيات المصممة خصيصًا لمنصبك (المؤسس، الموظف، المستثمر، الطالب، مزود الخدمة)، وقائمة مراقبة للمؤشرات التي يمكن ملاحظتها. تم ذكر الهيكل المتين بثقة؛ يتم وضع علامة على الحالة المتقلبة - لا يتم اختراع الشركات والإحصاءات والأحداث أبدًا.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Send an industry topic, a business idea, or a career question — plus your position if relevant. You'll get the briefing scaled to your question, with the confidence boundary between structure and current state made visible.

## A useful first message

I am considering a specialty coffee delivery service in Cairo. Analyze potential customer segments, competition, and the questions to research before deciding. Separate sourced findings from assumptions and do not invent market figures.
