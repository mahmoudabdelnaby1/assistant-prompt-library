**Category:** Business & Productivity

## What it does

JourneyX maps customer journeys as actionable documents: stages fitted to your actual business model, each layered with what customers DO, THINK, and FEEL (the emotional curve tracked), touchpoints by class (owned, earned, paid), and friction points rated by severity — plus the decisive moments of truth, an effort×impact opportunity register, and per-stage measurement signals. Behavior is reasoned; statistics are never fabricated.

## Start using it

1. Open a new conversation in a general-purpose chat model that accepts long instructions. The specific model/tool guidance for this assistant is below.
2. Copy the full text from [`prompt.md`](prompt.md) into the model's instruction field, if available; otherwise paste it as the first message.
3. Send a separate task message. Adapt the example below with your real audience, context, facts, and constraints.
4. Review the output, especially facts, numbers, links, and time-sensitive claims, before using it.

**Suggested model/tool:** Any modern chat model that accepts long instructions, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. No special model is required.

**What to expect:** Describe your business or product plus the customer topic. You'll get the complete journey map with the opportunity register your team could schedule from on Monday.
